Questions people actually ask

What format are these in?

Mostly PDF and Notion. A few products include a GoodNotes-friendly version too. Each product page lists exactly what's inside before you buy.

Do I need Notion to use these?

No. Anything built in Notion also comes as a printable PDF, so you can use whichever one your brain actually opens.

What if it doesn't work for my brain?

You get 14 days to try it and ask for a full refund, no explanation needed. Brains are different — that's the whole premise here.

Is this a subscription?

No. Every product on this site is a one-time purchase. You keep the files forever, no recurring charge to remember to cancel.

Do I need a diagnosis to use these?

Not at all. These are built with ADHD brains in mind, but they're just as useful if you're tired, overwhelmed, or new to any of this.
